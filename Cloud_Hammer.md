# Cloud Hammer
The Cloud Hammer Path cultivates water and air aura. Cloud Hammer Sacred Artists have a strong command of their aura and bring devastating physical offense and defense to a fight. They a respected, honorable sect favoring physicality over everything else. 


# Type(s) of Aura

Water plus air aura In the correct ratio combine with the Sacred Artists influence creates Cloud Hammer Madra
