# White Fox
The Path of the White Fox cultivates Light and Dream aura. White Fox Sacred Artists use illusions to tie up and distract the opponent while they accomplish their true goal.

This path is not often suite for combat because of the illusory nature of its techniques.

# Type(s) of Aura

The path of the White Fox takes advantage of Dream and Light aura. Using the ephemeral properties of both to create illusions to trick and lure opponents.
