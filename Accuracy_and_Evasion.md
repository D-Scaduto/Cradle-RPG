# Accuracy and Evasion
In Cradle RPG all attacks made by Sacred Artists are considered to have 100% accuracy against their target and will hit the defender every time, with two exceptions.

1. If a [Technique](Techniques.md) specifies an accuracy percent other than 100%
2. If the defender has Evasion greater than 0%

Accuracy is always measure in a a percentage and represents the number that a Sacred artist must roll UNDER when making an Accuracy check. Accuracy cannot go below zero but CAN go above 100%. If a [Technique](Techniques.md) or other attack would have an accuracy of 0% it is a guaranteed miss. Multiple forms of improved Accuracy stack.

Evasion is the opposing force to Accuracy. An evasive target is trying to avoid being hit and their evading talents will count towards dodging incoming attacks.

Evasion also comes in the form of percentage. Evasion cannot go below 0% but CAN go above 100%. Multiple forms of Evasion stack.

When calculating if an attack is capable of hitting take the Accuracy of the attacker and subtract the Evasion of the defender. If the result is 0% or less, the attack is a miss. If the result is 100% or greater the attack is a hit

**Example 1**:

Attackers Accuracy= 100%  
Defenders Evasion = 0%  
100%-0% = 100% chance to hit

**Example 2**:

Attackers Accuracy= 50%  
Defenders Evasion = 30%  
50%-30% = 20% chance to hit (The attacker must roll a d100 and have a result less than or equal 20. They can also roll a d10 and have the result less than or equal 2)

**Example 3**:

Attackers Accuracy= 150%  
Defenders Evasion = 110%  
150%-110% = 40% chance to hit (The attacker must roll a d100 and have a result less than or equal 40. They can also roll a d10 and have the result less than or equal 4)

**Example 4**:

Attackers Accuracy= 50%  
Defenders Evasion = 50%  
50%-50% = 0% chance to hit. The attack misses the target
