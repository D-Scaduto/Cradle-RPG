# Jobs
All of these are methods to gain raw ingredients, refined works, or simple make money by selling your services. 
## Soulsmithing
Crafting powerful weapons and tools to help with any task a Sacred Artist might encounter.

## Refining
Distilling, condensing, and concentrating the essence of the natural world into it's most potent forms. Essential for creating healing elixirs and advancement pills.

## Spirit Fruit Farming 
One of the primary sources of ingredients for Soulsmiths and Refiners. Instead of waiting for plants to harvest natural aura over hundreds of thousands of year, these people infuse the soil and their plants with their own madra speeding the process up. The plants grow as quickly as normal, mundane plants but they are as potent as ancient ones.

## Bounty Hunting
The other primary source of ingredients for Soulsmiths and Refiners. Sacred Beasts can be broken down into their base components and recombine into something of the creators desires. But many Soulsmiths and Refiners will need to source their ingredients from somewhere, they don't always have the time to go hunting. Bounty Hunters kill or capture these creatures for a living.
