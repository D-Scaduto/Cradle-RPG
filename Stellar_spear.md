# Stellar Spear
Using Stellar Spear Madra, a variation on Sword Madra, launch devastating Striker and Forger Techniques for an oppressive offense. Always attempting to keep your opponent on the back foot. Aiming beams of light for picking off long range targets, or summoning multiple spears of light to attack the opponent from every angle at once. Their techniques are enhanced when they work together, often seeing the most success when working together.

# Type(s) of Aura
Light and Sword Madra mixed together to form a deadly combination.
