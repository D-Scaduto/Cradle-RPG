# Paths
Paths should represent a philosophy that your character believes in and follows with the knowledge and faith they will get stronger by "following this path"

Paths can be as general or specific as you want them to be. A more general Path might be good for utility, And a more specific Path might be good to focus your character's talents and allow them to go grow stronger with each advancement.

Paths are also a mini biography. Public knowledge of a path might reveal to others your personal history, hardships, or possibly familial relationships. Walking a famous path has benefits and draw backs

Coordinate with your GM regarding your Path and the possible [Techniques](Techniques.md) that you want to learn as you grow stronger.

# Creating your own Path Manual

The heart of Cradle RRPG is to forge your own Path. Below are the required aspects of a Path and some guidelines and prompts to come up with your very own.

## The Path's Purpose

When creating a new Path it can be helpful to start with the central goal. What do you hope to achieve by practicing this Path? Total destruction? Helping others?

## Vital Aura

Now that you know what the goal(s) of the Path its time to find the right [Vital Aura](Madra.md#types-of-aura). We have only listed a few types of Aura, so feel free to be creative here and find the right aura that matches the Path.

## Iron Body

Every Path should have the perfect [Iron Body](Iron_Body.md) that compliments the goal Path. It should make the techniques that much stronger, allow the user to withstand the demands of the Path's techniques, or make up for inherent weaknesses that come with the Path.

Coming up with an Iron Body bonus should be thought of as a minor bonus that grows over time. It should start small, maybe even weak, that grows with the Sacred Artist. This where a Sacred Artist draws their physics strength and abilities.

If the Iron Body bonus can't increase [Life-Force](Life_Force.md), but it can increase [Damage Reduction](How_To_Play.md#damage-reduction) and Life Force regeneration.

Mechanically speaking, increasing Damage Reduction too much will create an invincible Sacred Artist. Damage Reduction should increment sparingly. +1 Damage Reduction per stage of advancement would be perfectly within reason. If the Path promotes large Damage Reduction, +5 per stage of advancement, it must come with an equally devastating cost such as the inability to use [Striker Techniques](Techniques.md#striker-s).

## Cycling Technique

Every Path must finalize their [Cycling Technique](Cycling_Technique.md) by the time they reach [Jade](Advancement.md#jade). And just like the Iron Body this should compliment the Path's goal. What is the best way to control your [Madra](Madra.md)?

Some prompts to think about how to best suit your Path:

- Do you want to focus on capacity?
- Recovery?
- Do you want to specialize in spending large amounts of Marda at once for large bursts of speed and power?
- Does your Path use numerous Auras, in a complex pattern? Maybe you should focus on filtering out the specific aspects the Path needs

In game terms your Cycling Technique should be about where the Sacred Artist exemplifies their spiritual abilities and talents. This is another bonus that will grow over time with the Sacred Artist. If the Path requires large resevoirs of Madra and the cycling technique is intended to increase capacity it shouldn't make it "infinite" it should just make it above average. Using the dice rolling technique described in [Character Creation](Character_Creation.md) has an average Madra capacity of 50 for a low gold. so an above average Madra capacity might make it 50% larger with a few advancements and double it by the time the Sacred Artist is in the [Lord stages](Advancement.md#lord).
