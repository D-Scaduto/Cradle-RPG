# Techniques

There are four different categories that all techniques can fit in. **Striker[S], Enforcer[E], Forger[F], and Ruler[R]**.

These are broad categorizations that are used to improve communication of techniques between Sacred Artist, but actual techniques do not have to be confined to these categories. As an example, if you wanted to develop an technique that changed the air around your body would that be an Enforcer[E] or a Ruler[R]. A case can be made for both depending on the intent behind the technique and the method it was developed. Speak with your Game Master about this to make sure you are both on the same _path_.

# Technique category

All Techniques known by a Sacred Artist must be assigned a category. Because all Sacred Artist has limited time they lean towards master a few Techniques instead of knowing numerous weak Techniques. For this reason all Techniques will only take up a single slot of a characters available Technique slots.

## Striker [S]

Strike Techniques are ranged techniques. Their goal is to manipulate something at a distance. The distance can vary from a few inches to a miles. For most Striker techniques the further away you attempt to hit, the weaker it will be.

## Enforcer [E]

Enforcer Techniques attempt to enhance the self or an object wielded by the Sacred Artist. This includes improving the hardness of your skin to increase defenses, making the weapon you're swinging heavier, therefore hitting harder, etc.

This can only be done to physical, tangible objects or the self.

## Forger [F]

Forger Techniques use Madre to manifest physical objects. Creating bricks out of thin air to construct a house, or manifesting water to ensnare your opponent.

## Ruler [R]

Ruler Techniques involved manipulating Madre outside of your body. This often includes illusion or light and darkness based techniques.

# Using Techniques at Lower Levels

Techniques can be used at lower levels of advancement for a reduced cost. It will reduce the cost by half rounded up. If a Technique is cast this way it will not affect the [Global Modifier](Global Modifier.md) but it will affect the [Level Difference Modifier](Level Difference Modifier.md)

# Using Techniques at Higher Levels

Similar to using a Technique at a lower level, you can also use a Technique at higher level at a large cost to you. You must pay the Technique's base cost in [Life Force](Life_Force.md) as well as doubling the base Madra cost

# Technique Properties

| Name                                 |Notes|
|---|---|
| Description                          |Brief description of the technique|
| Effect                               |Lists any special effect and [Damage](How_To_Play.md#damage) the technique is capable of|
| [Madra](Madra.md) Cost               |Numerical cost to use the Technique|
| [Range](How_To_Play.md#range)        |Melee, Close, Far|
| Honing Effect                        |This effect happens every time the Technique is honed. The most common effect is a Madra cost|
| Max times the Technique can be Honed |Some techniques can only be Honed so far before they Reach their max potential. It's These are often **Enforcer [E]** and **Forger [F]** Techniques that have a low mastery ceiling.|

# Honing a Technique

When spending honing a Technique there are several categories that most Sacred Artists can specialize in. Each type of upgrade has a specific point cost associated with it.

| Name                                    | Notes                                                                                                                               |
| --------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Strength                                | Increased the [Damage](How_To_Play.md#damage) done by a fixed amount (For example: +5 Damage)                                       |
| Intensity/Explosiveness                 | Increased the Damage done by a variable amount (For example: +1d10 Damage)                                                          |
| Increased [Range](How_To_Play.md#range) | Increase the Range of a Technique from Melee -> Close or from Close -> Far. Techniques can't be used at a distance greater than Far |
| Reduced [Madra](Madra.md) Cost          |                                                                                                                                     |
| Special Effects                         | Some Techniques can be improved by adding unique effects. Commonly seen in **Ruler [R]** and **Forger [F]** Techniques              |
