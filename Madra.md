# Madra
Madra is a pool of energy, or vital aura you have gathered over time. It is contained in your core and used to push your body above and beyond its normal limits. It also allows you to fire off advanced [techniques](Techniques.md) for a variety of effects.

## Max Madra

Each Sacred Artists is born with some starting Madra capacity. When they reach Jade and establish an advanced [Cycling Technique](Cycling_Technique.md) will begin to grow their maximum Madra. It's possible to store more Madra in your core than the maximum your character can typically store, but its only lasts for the day and comes at a painful cost, a temporary reduction to max [Life Force](Life_Force.md). The Life Force penalty is equal to the amount of Madra you are storing ABOVE your max Madra.

For example if your Max Madra is 50 and you are storing 60, the difference of 10 will reduce your max Life Force

## Madra Regeneration

Because you consume Madra daily, and especially when you use techniques, you're character will passively recover Madra. This is your [Passive Madra Regeneration](Madra.md#Passive-Madra-Regeneration). You can also actively [cycle](How_To_Play.md#cycling) which will restore you're Madra at rate equal to your Passive Madra Regeneration for every hour of cycling. Meaning you can recover quicker if you take the time to rest.

### Passive Madra Regeneration

Passive Madra Regeneration represents your body's ability to regenerate itself over time. After using a [Technique](Techniques.md) or 2, a Sacred Artist normally doesn't need to immediately [cycle](How_To_Play.md#cycling) and instead will passively gather vital aura over time.

A Sacred Artists Passive Madra Regeneration determines how much Madra the regenerate in a single day.

## Types of Aura

Aura comes in many forms: Fire aura, Water Aura, Destruction aura, and Force aura to name a few. Because of the metaphysical properties of Fire it often produces more than just Fire aura. Light Aura and Destruction aura will also be present.

Some locations are Aura hot spots that have accumulated a large amount of one kind of aura over centuries. These locations give a large boost to cycling for Sacred Artists on a corresponding path.

Some types of Aura like Life an Death might be conventionally thought of as  
opposites, but that's not the case. Aura accumulates in the presence of that aspect. A graveyard might have a lot of Death Aura, where a bustling city may have a lot of Life Aura. A volcano island will have Water Aura from the surrounding body of water plus Fire from the volcano. If the volcano were to erupt but there is nothing near the volcano that could be "destroyed" then there wouldn't be any destruction aura. However, if the volcano erupted and leveled an entire town, the air would be dense with destruction aura.

Aura can accumulate in certain geographical spots over time and the unique properties of each location contribute to that accumulation. Water aura from the ocean is not the same as that from a river, or, a large lake. Doubly so if there are living creatures in the water. These unique properties contribute to the types Madra absorbed by Sacred Artists and the Paths that they  practice. Helping differentiate two paths that use the same Madra, but in subtly different ways,

## Cycling

Cycling the main way Sacred Artists improve their [Madra capacity](Madra.md#max-madra) and recover from battle. Cycling and Madra are directly tied together. Over the course of a Sacred Artists life they will meditate and Cycle numerous times. This is a time of reflection and replenishment, intended to help grow a Sacred Artist.

See [Cycling Technique](Cycling_Technique.md) and [How to Play](How_To_Play.md#cycling) for more information on the specifics of Cycling.
