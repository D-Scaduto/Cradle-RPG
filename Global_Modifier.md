# Global Modifier

The Global Modifier represents how strong you have grown over time. This a boost to EVERYTHING that you attempt to do. This will be added to your damage, to your [Ruler Saving Throw](How_To_Play.md#ruler-saving-throws), to your [Damage Reduction](How_To_Play.md#damage-reduction), to your [Skill Checks](How_To_Play.md#Skill-Checks), to everything!
