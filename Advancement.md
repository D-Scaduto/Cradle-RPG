# Advancement
As some who follows the Sacred Arts, you will go through many physical, psychological, and emotional changes as you progress down your [Path](Paths.md).

There are 11 stages of advancement, and anyone below a Gold is likely a child who is just starting out on their journey.

Can use this page for detailed explanation [https://abidan-archives.fandom.com/wiki/Ranks](https://abidan-archives.fandom.com/wiki/Ranks)

# Advancement Stages

1. Copper
2. Iron
3. Jade
4. Low Gold
5. High Gold
6. True Gold
7. Underlord + Underlady
8. Overlord + Overlady
9. Archlord + Archlady
10. Sage / Herald
11. Monarch

# Copper

Children reach this stage when they are able to "open their copper sight" allowing them to see various Madra in the world.

# Iron

Children and teenagers have continued to train and cycle to accumulate enough Madra and condense their core.

This stage also include receiving an [Iron Body](Iron_Body.md). This can be accomplished any way, but the thoroughness and intensity of the procedure to obtain a specific Iron Body often dictates how powerful it is for a given Sacred Artist.

# Jade

The next step in a Sacred Artist Journey is again requires condensing their core allowing them to store even more Madra.

# Gold

Sacred Artists who have reached this stage in their careers understand the nuance of advancement and designate the next three stages as sub categories leading up to the [Lord](Advancement.md#Lord) stage

## Low Gold

- See [Gold Sign](Gold_Sign.md).  
- Players will always create characters starting at this stage [Character Creation](Character_Creation.md)

This is where a Sacred Artist Journey truly begins. They have accumulated a sufficient amount of life experience to enable them to venture into the world and develop their [Techniques](Techniques.md) on their own. A Sacred Artist might also start to alter an existing [Path](Paths.md) to be their own.

## High Gold

High Gold is a refinement on the existing gold foundation. With this stage comes greater control over your [Gold Sign](Gold_Sign.md).

## True Gold

True gold has perfect control over their  [Gold Sign](Gold_Sign.md) and is starting to make preparations to advance to Underlord. Making it to True Gold is a great accomplishment and many leaders are respected as True Golds. Their power is not to be underestimated.

# Lord

Lord stages each include different manipulations of [Soulfire](Soulfire.md). Additionally each of these stages can only be reached with a specific revelation unique to an individual and a specific details about their psyche.

## [Soulfire](soulfire.md)

As part of the Lord stages you will gain the ability to wield [Soulfire](Soulfire.md).

## Underlord + Underlady

Underlords and ladies have just opened up their Soulspace and can store [Soulfire](Soulfire.md). To reach this stage a Sacred Artist must reason out intrinsic self truths and find your Underlord Revelation

## Overlord + Overlady

Overlords and ladies are often directly related to the most powerful beings in the nation and are possibly being groomed to become the next family Sage or Herald.

## Archlord + Archlady

Peak Lords. Have mastered their body and mind to an unparalleled level. Have Also travelled so far down a [Path](Paths.md) they have attracted the attention of higher powers and are certainly considered a threat if left unchecked.

## Lord Revelations

Finding the true answer to each of these questions is required to advance to the next stage.

**Underlord Revelation**

- Why did I first begin to follow the Sacred Arts? 
- What pushed me forward?

**Overlord Revelation**

- Who am I today? 
- How do others see me?

**Archlord Revelation**

- Where am I going? 
- Who will I become, as a Sacred Artist?

# Sage

Sage is someone who has manifested an Icon of Authority. Often a peak Archlord or lady.

# Herald

Advancement occurs typically by fusing with one’s own Remnant, thus becoming half physical and half spirit, but there have been other more unique cases.

# Monarch

Monarch is when a Sacred Artist both manifest an Icon of Authority and has fused with their own remnant. They are both a Sage and Herald.
