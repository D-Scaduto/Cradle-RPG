# Iron Body
The process by which your body is re-forged to be better suited to handle your own personal [Path](Paths.md).

### Iron Body Benefits

Depending on your [Path's](Paths.md) Iron Body, you may get bonuses from these categories

1. [Damage Reduction](How_To_Play.md#damage-reduction)
2. [Life Force](Life_Force.md) Regeneration
3. Resistance to [Ailments](ailments.md)
4. Physical Strength - the ability to lift heavy things
5. Improved stamina
