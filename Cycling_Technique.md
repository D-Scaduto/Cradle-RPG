# Cycling Technique

When a Sacred Artist reaches jade, the develop a complex cycling technique that will allow them to advance past the basic stages. The Cycling Technique is a Madra spinning pattern that has two effects" 1) making the Madra in your core more dense, and 2) increasing your capacity.

Many [Paths](Paths.md) recommend, or require, a specific Cycling Technique. This is your character's way to grow their core and increase their Madra supply and control.

## Cycling Technique Bonuses

Depending on your Path's Cycling Technique, you may get bonuses from these categories

1. Max Capacity of [Madra](Madra.md)
2. [Madra](Madra.md) regeneration
3. [Techniques](Techniques.md) cost reduction
4. Improved [Techniques](Techniques.md) honing capabilities
5. More [Techniques](Techniques.md) slots
6. Improve your [Iron Body](Iron_Body.md) bonuses
