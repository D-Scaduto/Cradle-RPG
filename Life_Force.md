# Life Force
Life Force is your ability to stand up and continue to fight! The higher your Life Force, the stronger you feel. When you take [damage](How_To_Play.md#Damage) you lose Life Force. You can also Rest to recover your Life Force. Some Sacred Artists choose to use techniques that boost their recovery and speed up this processes.

Your Life Force can never go above its maximum value. Only small number or [Iron Bodies](Iron_Body.md) and [Cycling techniques](Cycling_Technique.md) give the ability to increase your maximum Life Force.

## Resting

Like all humans, Sacred Artists require rest and sleep to recover from injuries. After a good nights rest a Sacred Artist will recover 25% of their Life Force, healing back to 100% in a maximum of 4 days.
