# Gold Sign
The Gold Sign a physical change to a Sacred Artist when they absorb a remnant as part of advancing to [Gold](Advancement.md#gold). It can also come about through a pact with a Sacred Beast.

Two example are Yerin's Sword arm(s) that she got by absorbing her master's remnant. And Lindon's eyes after making a pact with Orthos.

## Use and Effects

Many Gold Signs are a mark of the clan of family you belong to; displaying to others that you are a powerful Sacred Artist. Some Gold Signs are dangerous to others, some to yourself. They don't have to be visible either.

If a twisted, warped bone remnant were absorb, a Gold Sign might be changing of the bone structure and joints which isn't obvious on the surface but becomes obvious when the person has more or fewer joints than normal giving them inhuman movements.

## Character Creation

When deciding on a Gold Sign as part of [Character Creation](Character_Creation.md) it can be simply aesthetic or have some use towards your path. Sometimes the sign you chose isn't an obvious boon or hinderance, but it becomes more obvious as your character develops.

If you come from a wealthy back ground and were able to ascend to [Gold](Advancement.md#gold) solely through elixirs and pills than you would not receive a Gold Sign. A Gold Sign is the representation of the Madra of another being inside you. If there is no other being than there is no Gold Sign

## Making a Pact

If you make pact with a Sacred Beast, you will have an ally who you share Madra with. There are obvious boons to this type of connection, but there are also subtle draw backs.

If the Sacred Beast is very strong their power may overwhelm you until advance further. If their power has a particularly deadly aspect, and your Iron Body doesn't help withstand these effects, you may have suffer from [Life Force](Life_Force.md) or [Madra](Madra.md) penalties
